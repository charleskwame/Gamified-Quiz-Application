# Application Evaluation Questions

Rate each question below on a scale of 1 to 5.
1 means the lowest / most negative rating. 5 means the highest / most positive rating.
Each question shows what 1 and 5 mean.

## 1. General / Overall Experience

1. How satisfied are you with the overall quiz application experience? (1 = Very dissatisfied, 5 = Very satisfied)
2. How likely are you to recommend this app to a friend or colleague? (1 = Very unlikely, 5 = Very likely)
3. How would you rate the visual design and quality of the app? (1 = Poor, 5 = Excellent)
4. How likely are you to come back and use the app again? (1 = Very unlikely, 5 = Very likely)
5. How well does the app meet your expectations? (1 = Not at all, 5 = Very well)

6. How easy is navigation around the app? (1 = Very hard, 5 = Very easy)
7. How easy is it to understand the icons and labels? (1 = Very confusing, 5 = Very clear)
8. How easy is it to start your first quiz? (1 = Very hard, 5 = Very easy)
9. How easy is it to understand the question text and answer options? (1 = Very hard, 5 = Very easy)
10. How easy is it to view past results and history? (1 = Very hard, 5 = Very easy)

## 2. Usability

11. How accessible is the app for users with different abilities? (1 = Very poor, 5 = Very good)
12. How helpful is the onboarding or tutorial when you first use the app? (1 = Not helpful, 5 = Very helpful)
13. How consistent and clear is the layout across screens? (1 = Very confusing, 5 = Very clear)
14. How obvious is it what to tap or do next on each screen? (1 = Confusing, 5 = Always clear)

## 3. Performance

15. How fast does the app start up? (1 = Very slow, 5 = Very fast)
16. How smooth do the screens and animations feel? (1 = Very janky, 5 = Very smooth)
17. How quickly do questions load when a quiz starts? (1 = Very slow, 5 = Very fast)
18. How stable is the app (crashes, freezes, or hangs)? (1 = Very unstable, 5 = Very stable)
19. How responsive is the app to taps? (1 = Very laggy, 5 = Very responsive)
20. How well does the app handle weak or steady internet connections? (1 = Very poorly, 5 = Very well)
21. How easy is it to tell the state of loading or saving? (1 = Very confusing, 5 = Very clear)

## 4. Engagement

22. How motivating is the app to keep you playing? (1 = Not motivating, 5 = Very motivating)
23. How often do you feel like coming back for another quiz session? (1 = Almost never, 5 = Very often)
24. How much do you enjoy a typical quiz session? (1 = Not at all, 5 = Very much)
25. How often do you play several quizzes one after the other? (1 = Rarely, 5 = Very often)
26. How invested do you feel in your profile and progress? (1 = Not at all, 5 = Very much)

30. How easy is it to discover the features? (1 = Very hard, 5 = Very easy)

33. How much control do you get over quiz options, such as topic or difficulty? (1 = Very little, 5 = Great deal)
